 A RAG application combines retrieval-based and generation-based models to produce 
more accurate and contextually relevant responses. It retrieves relevant documents from 
a knowledge base and uses a language model to generate responses based on the retrieved 
information.

 Ollama provides robust language models that can be integrated with Langflow's 
flexible workflow management system. Using Ollama with Langflow allows you to leverage 
powerful language models in a streamlined, configurable environment for building RAG 
applications.
